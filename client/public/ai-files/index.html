Use the tool files with your AI

Every Praxis tool comes as a file you can give to your own AI. Drop it into your folder, start a chat and your AI walks you through the same steps as the tool on the site.

Step 01

Get the files

Each tool has a button at the top marked "Download for your AI (.md)". They're also on your dashboard at tutto.one/learn, next to each tool.

A .md file is plain text with a little formatting. Each one has two parts: a short note for you at the top, and the instructions your AI follows to run the tool.

Step 02

Put them in your working folder

Keep the files where your AI works, so it can read them whenever you need them.

  1. Open your working folder in VS Code (step 2 of the setup guide shows how).
  2. Drag the downloaded files from your Downloads folder onto the Explorer on the left.
  3. They appear in the list with the rest of your files, as in Fig. 1. Click one to read it. To see it formatted, choose Markdown Preview from the menu at the top right of the file.
Step 03

Copy the file's path

The path tells your AI exactly which file to read.

Step 04

Start a chat and say what you want

Open the AI panel on the right, start a new chat, paste the path and say in a sentence or two what you want to make with it.

Say what you're working on. The file is enough to start, but a line about your real task means your AI tailors its suggestions from the first question.

Not using VS Code? In Claude, ChatGPT, Le Chat, Gemini or Copilot, open a new chat, attach the file with the paperclip or + button (or paste everything in it), and send Let's start with a line about your task. Everything else works the same.

Step 05

Let your AI guide you

It runs the same steps as the tool on the site: one step at a time, with suggestions and the same documents at the end.

  • Short answers are fine: "yes", "2 and 4", "skip".
  • You can go back to any step by saying so.
  • If your AI can create documents, it offers to put the final result in one. If not, it writes it out in the chat for you to copy.
Step 06

Test what you made

Answer the questions and let your AI guide you through. Tell it if parts aren't relevant to your task. Then test the result, ideally on something where you already know the answer.

Showing it what good work looks like beats describing it, and doing both gives you the best results. Keep a couple of finished pieces of real work to hand, and ask your AI to check its output against them.

Your answers stay in your AI's chat, under that tool's terms. Leave out personal or confidential details unless your organisation has approved the tool. If your agent can act on its own, keep it on the mode that asks before it acts (Manual in Claude, Ask in Mistral) until you trust it.
