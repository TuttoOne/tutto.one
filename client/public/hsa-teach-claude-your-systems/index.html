Teach Claude your systems

Christine split a learner guide into activities and Claude wrote a Moodle question file for it. It loaded, after a struggle. The way to get it right first time is the same for Moodle, your bank or any other system: show Claude a real file that came out of that system, and it copies the pattern exactly.

Start from a file the system made

Build one example by hand in Moodle and check it looks right. Then export it and give that file to Claude as the template. The file holds settings you'd never think of, like where a text box sits, and the template carries them over.

Which file for which Moodle job

Check with your Moodle person which of these your site allows and how big a file can be, before you make a lot of anything.

You wantThe fileNotes
Questions in the question bankMoodle XMLCarries hints, feedback and every setting. GIFT and Aiken are simpler formats for plain multiple choice.
Something interactive: drag, sort, hotspots, branchingH5PMoodle runs H5P itself. Elsa's inspection picture could become an H5P hotspot activity.

The same trick works everywhere

Almost every system lets you download a file. One real example teaches Claude more than a page of description.

Finance

A bank statement export (CSV) for a cash-flow analysis or a board note.

Moodle

A gradebook or course export, with learner names and numbers taken out first.

Council

Last quarter's video list check, so this quarter's comes out in the same layout.

Anything with a template

Download the system's own blank template, fill one row by hand, then give it to Claude.

Let Claude use your browser

With the Claude in Chrome extension, Claude opens a tab in your own browser, asks your permission, then reads and clicks through sites you're already signed into, and reports back in the chat.

Good first jobs
  • Open every link on the Council video list and say which no longer play
  • Read a Moodle course's settings and list what differs from another course
  • Collect the current fees from the SAPC and QCTO pages
Not for it
  • Anything with learner records
  • Payments, passwords or banking
  • Submitting anything on HSA's behalf

Look under the page

Every web page has code underneath it. In Chrome, right-click anything and choose Inspect, and a panel opens showing the code for that part of the page, lighting up as your mouse moves over it. That's what Claude reads when it works in your browser. When you want a Moodle page or an artifact to look like another one, a screenshot is a good start. Copying the part of the page you want from that panel and pasting it into the chat is more exact.

What to ask IT

You're starting to do things IT normally looks after. Ask once in writing and keep the answers in your project.

  • Sharing outside HSA. Elsa can't share artifacts outside HSA. Who may share with whom, and who decides?
  • Claude in Chrome. May we install the extension, and on which sites may it work?
  • Connectors. OneDrive and Outlook: which folders and mailboxes, and for whom?
  • The Moodle test site. Who has access, and who copies a checked course to live?
  • Learner data. What may go into Claude, under which account, agreed with FPD under POPIA.

We can join that conversation. If it helps, Daniel can talk to FPD's IT team directly so you don't have to translate.
