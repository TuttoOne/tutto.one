From a paragraph to a clip

Elsa's clip on the Council grading system and Laetitia's one on hazardous substances show what you can already make. This page is the route from one section of a learner guide to something a learner watches or clicks, with the tool for each step and the checks on the way.

The route, every time

Six steps. Claude does most of the making, you do the choosing and the checking, and nothing reaches a learner until Elsa and Christine have seen it.

Which tool for which job

Start with what you already have in Claude. Reach for a paid specialist tool only when a clip needs something Claude can't draw.

Brief a video tool shot by shot

Video tools get confused by a big brief. Give them one short shot at a time, each with the same reference picture so the look stays the same, then join the shots. This is Christine's Stock Receiving idea as four shots.

One HSA video style

Write the look down once, the way Laetitia did for documents with the design system, and every clip comes out looking like it belongs to the same course.

Look

HSA colours and fonts from the design system. Flat illustrations, no realistic people in Claude clips. Captions at the bottom, 8 words at most.

Length

30 to 60 seconds, one idea per clip. Works with the sound off and on a slow connection.

Sound

One South African English voice for the whole course. Soft music or none: Laetitia's organ music can go.

Close

The last frame names the module and the learner guide page, so a learner can go and read it.

Whose video is it?

The video list you check for the Council every quarter keeps losing links, and learners struggle with some of the accents. Before you change or re-voice any video you didn't make, walk this.

Add a question after every clip

A clip on its own is watched once and forgotten. Two or three questions straight after it, each with hints that lead the learner to the answer instead of handing it over, is where they learn it. A tutor that doesn't give the answer shows how to write those hints once and reuse them for every learner.

Prompts to copy

1 · Storyboard firstAttached is the learner guide section on [topic]. I want a 45-second clip for pharmacist's assistant learners. Before you make anything, write a storyboard as a table: scene, seconds, what's on screen, the caption (8 words at most), the narration line and the learner guide page it comes from. Use only what's in the section. If a scene needs a fact that isn't there, write [CHECK]. Then stop and wait for me.
2 · Build itBuild the storyboard as a Motion clip. Follow @hsa-video-style.md. Keep each scene to the seconds in the storyboard. Here are two pictures of the look I want: [attach].
3 · Brief a video toolI want to make shot [1] of this storyboard in [Midjourney / Sora / Higgsfield]. Look up how that tool is best briefed, then write the brief for this one shot: what the camera sees, how it moves, the light, and how long it lasts. It must match the attached reference picture. Keep it short: that tool works better with less.
4 · Our video style, written onceLook at these 3 clips we've made and our design system. Write hsa-video-style.md: colours, fonts, illustration style, caption rules, clip length, voice, music and the closing frame. Ask me about anything you can't tell from the examples.
