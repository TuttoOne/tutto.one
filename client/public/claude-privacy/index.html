Privacy guide

Stop Claude training on your chats

On a personal Claude plan, Anthropic asks whether it can use your chats and coding sessions to train its models. One switch in Settings turns that off, in the desktop app, on claude.ai and in Claude Code.

Step 01

Why switch it off

With the switch on, what you type into Claude can end up in the data Anthropic trains its next models on, and Anthropic keeps those chats for up to 5 years. With it off, they're kept for 30 days and stay out of training.

That matters as soon as you work with real material: a client's figures, a candidate's CV, a draft contract, the files Claude Code reads in your VS Code folder. The setting covers your chats and your coding sessions, so the one switch also covers Claude Code when you're signed in with the same account (see the VS Code setup guide).

On a Team or Enterprise plan, or using Claude through the API? Those run under Anthropic's commercial terms, and your chats aren't used for training. You may not see this switch at all. If in doubt, ask whoever manages your company's Claude account.

Step 02

Open Settings

Open the Claude app and click your name in the bottom-left corner.

Step 03

Switch off Help improve our AI models

In Settings, click Privacy in the list on the left. The switch is under Preferences.

The change saves straight away, so you can close Settings. It follows your account, which means it applies on claude.ai, in the phone app and in Claude Code too.

Location metadata sits just above. It lets Claude use your rough location (city or region) to tailor answers. It has nothing to do with training, so leave it on or off as you prefer.

Step 04

What the switch does and doesn't cover

It works from now on. A few things sit outside it, and they're worth knowing before you put client material into Claude.

  • New and resumed chats: from the moment you switch it off, Anthropic doesn't use new chats and coding sessions for training, or old chats you carry on.
  • What's already been used stays used. If the switch was on before, chats already taken into training can't be pulled back out of models that are trained or in training.
  • Thumbs up and thumbs down: rating a reply sends that conversation to Anthropic as feedback, and feedback can be used and kept for up to 5 years whatever the switch says. Don't rate chats that hold client material.
  • Incognito chats aren't used for training either way, and they don't stay in your chat history.
  • Deleting a chat removes it from your history, and from what can be used later.

Switching it off is about training. Anthropic still processes and stores your chats to run the service (30 days by default). Keep to your organisation's rules on what you can put into an AI tool at all, and use the practice folder from the VS Code setup guide until you're comfortable.

If something is missing

  • No Help improve our AI models switch: you're probably on a Team or Enterprise plan, where chats aren't used for training. Check the plan name next to your name in the bottom-left corner.
  • The switch keeps turning itself back on: you probably have a second account, for example one signed in with Google and one with email. Check the email at the top of the account menu (Fig. 2) and switch it off there too.
  • You want a copy of what Anthropic holds: the same Privacy page has Export data under Your data.
