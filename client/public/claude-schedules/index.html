Scheduled tasks guide

Let Claude do the weekly jobs

A scheduled task is a job you describe once and Claude runs on its own, every morning, every Monday or whenever you click. The result waits for you in the app, and if it makes a page you can share it with your team by link.

Step 01

What a scheduled task is

Think of it as a standing instruction to a colleague: "every Monday at 7, go and look at this and leave me a short note".

You give the task a name, the instructions and how often it runs. When the time comes, Claude starts a fresh chat on its own, does the work and saves the result under Scheduled in the left sidebar. It can search the web, read the files and connected apps you've given it access to (your calendar, email, Drive and so on) and make a document or a page.

Good jobs for it are the ones that start with "go and look at...": checking for new regulations, a morning brief from your calendar and inbox, a weekly summary of what changed in a shared file, a reminder list for Friday.

Plans. Scheduled tasks are on every paid plan: Pro, Max, Team and Enterprise. On Team and Enterprise your admin can switch them off, so if you can't see Scheduled, ask whoever manages your Claude account.

Step 02

Do it once in a chat first

Before you put anything on a timetable, do the job once with Claude in an ordinary chat and check the result.

That tells you Claude can do it with what it has access to, and it shows you what to put in the instructions: the sources it should use, the format you want back and what it got wrong the first time. When the chat gives you something you'd be happy to receive every week, you're ready to schedule it.

Step 03

Three ways to set one up

All three end in the same place. Pick the one that suits you.

  1. Ask in any chat. In the newest version of the app you can just say it: "Every Monday at 7:00, check... and give me...". Answer Claude's questions, check the name, schedule and instructions it proposes, then click Schedule.
  2. Create with Claude. Click Scheduled in the left sidebar, then New task in the top right, then Create with Claude. Claude asks you a few questions, some with multiple-choice answers, and writes the task for you. Check it and click Schedule.
  3. Set up manually. Same start, but choose Set up manually and fill in the form yourself (Fig. 2). Good when you've already done the job in a chat and can paste in the instructions that worked.
Step 04

Write the instructions

Nobody is there to answer questions when the task runs, so the instructions have to say everything you'd say to a new colleague.

  • Where to look. Name the sites, files or apps. "Official sources only" is vague; "the Government Gazette and the Pharmacy Council website" is clear.
  • What to bring back. The format, the length and the fields: date, title, link, one line on why it matters.
  • What to do if there's nothing. "If there's nothing new, say so and stop." Otherwise it may fill the space.
  • What not to do. Don't send anything, don't change any files, don't guess a number.
  • A time guard. If the run happens late (step 05), "only look at the last 7 days" keeps it right.
Template · copy and fill in the amber parts 
[How often], look at [where: named sites, files or connected apps] for [what: anything new about..., anything due..., anything that changed...] in the last [7 days].
For each item give [the fields you want: date, title, link, one line on what it means for us].
Use only what you find in those sources. If something is unclear, say so instead of guessing.
If there's nothing new, say "Nothing new this week" and stop.
Don't send emails, change files or contact anyone.
Step 05

When it runs

Most tasks run on Anthropic's servers, so they run on time even when your laptop is asleep or the app is closed.

  • Tasks that need your computer are different. If you pick a folder on your computer or a job needs an app installed on it, the task can only run there, so the computer has to be on and the app open.
  • If your computer slept through a run, the app runs it once when you're back, for the most recent missed time only. A 7:00 task might run at 11:00. That's why the time guard in step 04 matters.
  • A task that stops to ask permission waits for you, and nothing happens until you answer. Run it once with Run now while you're at your desk and approve what it needs, so later runs don't get stuck.
  • It uses your plan. Every run counts towards your usage like a chat does. Hourly tasks add up fast, and weekly is plenty for most jobs.

Using Claude Code? The Code tab of the desktop app has its own scheduled tasks for work on your files, local ones that need the app open and cloud ones that don't. The setup is a little different and is covered in Anthropic's Claude Code docs.

Step 06

Look after your tasks

Click Scheduled in the sidebar, then click a task to open it.

New results show a small blue dot next to the task in the sidebar. Depending on your notification settings, you also get a pop-up when a run finishes.

Step 07

Share what it makes

When a task (or any chat) makes a page, a chart or a document, Claude saves it as an artifact. You'll find it under Artifacts in the sidebar, and you can share it from there.

  • Someone without Claude needs to edit it? Ask Claude to make a Word or PowerPoint version and work on that. The layout shifts a little between formats.
  • Need a PDF? The PDF export can break pages in odd places. Ask Claude in the chat instead: "make a PDF of this, with page breaks only before a heading".
  • Team and Enterprise plans may also offer "Anyone at your organisation", and your admin decides whether links can go outside it.

A link can be forwarded. Don't share a link to anything confidential, any personal data (names, ID numbers, marks, salaries) or a client's material. Invite named people instead, or export a file and send it the usual way. Check two numbers in anything a task made before someone else reads it.

Step 08

Ideas to start with

Start with one task, set to manual. Run it a few times by hand, fix the instructions, then put it on a timetable.

Morning briefWeekdays at 7:30: today's meetings, emails that need an answer and anything due this week.
Regulation or news watchWeekly: new notices on named official sites, with links or "nothing new".
Before a board or client meetingWhat changed in a shared file since last time, in one page.
Friday look-aheadWhat's due next week from a plan or timetable you keep in Claude.
Content ideasWeekly: three ideas for posts or lessons from what's new in your field, with sources.
HousekeepingMonthly: remind you to check your privacy setting and tidy your rules files.

If something is missing

  • No Scheduled in the sidebar: switch to the chat view (the speech bubble), look under More, and update the app. On Team and Enterprise, ask your admin whether it's switched on.
  • A task didn't run: open it and check the history. Tasks that use a folder on your computer only run while it's awake and the app is open.
  • A run is stuck: it's probably waiting for your permission. Open the run and answer it.
  • No Share button, or no "Anyone with the link": on Team and Enterprise your admin controls outside sharing. Sharing settings can't be changed in the phone app.
