Getting your content ready

Before the AI can help with the remap, it needs to know what each part of a guide is. Today we turn the Stock Receiving guides into clean text and tag every part, so in session 3 it can match old units to new modules and you can check each match.

When Mon 2 Nov 2026, 9:00 to 10:30 SAST Working example Stock Receiving

Three steps

From the guide as it is now to parts the AI can work with.

  1. Step 1

    Word guide

    The resource guide, learner guide and memo as they are now. Headings, tables, crosswords and all.

  3. Step 2

    Clean text

    Same words, no layout. One heading per unit, tables kept as plain lists, page furniture taken out.

  5. Step 3

    Tagged parts

    Every part carries a label saying where it sits in the qualification, what it teaches and how much it's worth.

The six tags

Where it sitsWhat it teachesWhat it's worth
moduleOld and new code, e.g. old KM-02, new KM-02
unitThe topic or unit inside the module
outcomeWhat the learner can do after it, in the curriculum's words
assessmentThe criteria it's assessed against, and how (MCQ, activity, workplace)
creditsFrom the curriculum document
hoursNotional hours. 1 credit is 10 hours

We agree the final list together today. If something in your guides doesn't fit a tag, write it down: that's what we need to see.

Three prompts for today

Copy them in order. Swap [X] for the section you're on.

  1. 1 · Summarise and checkSummarise section [X] of the Stock Receiving resource guide in about 150 words. After each point, quote the sentence from the guide it comes from.
  2. 2 · Tag the guideSplit this guide into its units. Give each part these tags: module, unit, outcome, assessment, credits, hours, source page. Use only what is in the guide and the curriculum. If a tag isn't there, write MISSING.
  3. 3 · Check the tagsFor every tag you filled in, show me the exact sentence in the guide or curriculum it came from, with the page number. Mark any tag you can't back up.

Before you trust it

  • Open the page it quotes and read the line yourself.
  • Check credits and hours against the curriculum, every time.
  • Keep learner names, ID numbers and marks out of it.
  • Mark anything the AI drafted as AI-assisted. You stay responsible for it.
