How we know the work is good

The AI writes the draft. Your scorecard decides whether it's good enough, and a person signs it off. When it fails, we fix the instructions, so the next run is better too.

An example scorecard, one run Extracting directorships from three registry PDFs into a table

1
No invented factsEvery name, date and share is on the page it cites
Pass
2
CompleteAll three PDFs read, and the row count matches what you'd expect
Pass
3
Correct dataFive rows picked at random, checked against the source
Fail
4
Right formatMatches the table the client is used to
Pass
5
Sounds like usReads like our past reports, no AI phrasing
Pass
Four out of five isn't a pass

One start date came from the wrong line of the extract. The draft goes back, and the fix goes into the instructions: "take dates from the appointment line, never the filing date".

  • "No invented facts" is always check number one.
  • Each check is something a colleague could tick without asking you.
  • Anything it can't find says [NOT IN SOURCE]. Anything it's unsure of says [CHECK].
  • Keep a line per run: date, minutes, errors found. In a month you'll know if it saves time.
