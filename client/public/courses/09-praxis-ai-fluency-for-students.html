Tutto.Lesson 1 · The frame
Lesson 1

Augmentation, not automation

Automation has AI do the work for you. Augmentation has it help you do your work better. In learning, only one of those leaves you stronger.

Fluency is using AI effectively, efficiently, ethically and safely — and choosing augmentation when the doing is the point. These are enduring principles, not prompt tricks: they hold however the tools change.

03
Tutto.Lesson 2 · The frame
Lesson 2 — if you take one thing away

The 4D Framework

Four habits that make any AI interaction sound: Delegation, Description, Discernment, Diligence. The middle two are a loop you run on repeat.

Delegation and Diligence bookend the work: decide what to hand over, then stand behind whatever you submit. Description and Discernment are the engine in between — say what you want, judge what returns, say it better. These four hold no matter how the technology evolves.

Tutto.Lesson 3 · In practice
Lesson 3

A coach, not a substitute player

The same prompt can do your homework or build your understanding. Steer AI to question and check rather than hand over answers.

Two tools from this lesson: a study buddy configured to question rather than answer, and a living journal that tracks what actually clicked. The test is simple — could you explain it in an exam, with no AI in the room?

05
Tutto.Lesson 4 · In practice
Lesson 4

Information from AI, authenticity from you

Use AI to research, structure and rehearse — never to manufacture a version of you. Here's the workflow, from CV to interview.

The same shape works for cover letters, skill-building and career mapping. Employers spot generic AI output instantly — so every word that represents you stays yours. And AI fluency itself is now a skill worth showing.

06
Tutto.Lesson 5 · The standard
Lesson 5

You're the one in the loop

You decide what to ask, judge whether the answer holds, and bring the values a model can't. Write that down as a policy you'll actually follow.

Being the human in the loop is a practice, not a one-off: keep asking whether AI is helping you grow, whether your skills are sharpening, whether you're being honest about its role. Your judgement, creativity and care stay irreplaceable.
