03Is this a good first AI use case?

You're looking for a task that matters enough to be worth it and is small enough to learn from. The most impressive one can wait.

CheckYesNo / unsureWhat to note
I can describe the task in one or two sentences.If not, narrow it.
The inputs and the expected output are reasonably clear.Vague in, vague out.
I do this often enough for an improvement to matter.One-off tasks are harder to learn from.
A person can review the result before it's used.Accountability stays with a person.
The task isn't mainly a high-stakes decision.Start with help on the work, leave the final call to people.
I can tell what "good" looks like.You need a quality target.

04Split the work: AI helps, you own it

A good use case rarely hands the whole task over. Decide where AI can help and where judgement and accountability stay with you.

AI could help with

For example: summarise, compare, sort, extract, draft, reformat, brainstorm, spot patterns.

I still have to

For example: give the business context, check facts and figures, decide, approve, handle the exceptions, take responsibility.

05Quick risk check

A "yes" doesn't rule the task out. It may mean a narrower task, safer data, a stronger review or a different place to start.

Does this task involveY / NSafeguard needed
Confidential, personal or commercially sensitive information
Financial figures, exact calculations, dates or citations that must be right
A legal, HR, safety, regulatory or other high-stakes decision
Communication with customers or the public that could affect your reputation
A result that's hard to check independently
An action AI should never take without a person's approval

06What would success look like?

"Make it better" and "save time" aren't enough. Describe a result you'd recognise and could compare.
