Tutto.Step 01
01 · 15 minutes

What eats your week?

List the recurring tasks that take time, cause friction or feel more manual than they should. Don't worry yet about whether AI is the answer.

Recurring taskHow oftenTimeWhat makes it slow
Landlord statement packMonthly1.5 daysLedger export, invoices and a cover note per landlord, all put together by hand
Chasing contractor quotesWeekly3 hoursEmails back and forth, quotes arrive in different formats
Answering tenant FAQsDaily45 minSame 20 questions, answers scattered across old emails
Example: Ana, operations lead at a lettings agency
04
Tutto.Step 02
02 · 15 minutes

Pick one task and make it concrete

Choose a task you do yourself. A good first one has a clear start, a clear output and someone who can say whether the result is good.

Task in one sentenceProduce the monthly statement pack for each landlord
What starts itMonth-end close in the property software, first working day of the month
InputsRent ledger export (Excel), maintenance invoices (PDF), last month's notes
OutputOne PDF per landlord: statement plus a short cover note explaining anything unusual
Who reviews itAna, then the branch manager for anything over £1,000
Where judgement is neededDeciding what counts as "unusual" and how to explain it to each landlord
Example: Ana, operations lead at a lettings agency
05
Tutto.Step 03
03 · 10 minutes

Is this a good first use case?

You're looking for a task that matters enough to be worth it and is small enough to learn from. The impressive one can wait.

CheckAnaIf it's a no
I can describe it in one or two sentencesYesNarrow it
Inputs and output are reasonably clearYesVague in, vague out
I do it often enough for it to matterYesOne-off tasks are harder to learn from
A person reviews the result before it's usedYesAdd a review step first
It isn't mainly a high-stakes decisionYesStart with the prep work around the decision
I can tell what "good" looks likeYesWrite down one good example first
06
Tutto.Step 04
04 · 10 minutes

AI helps. You own it.

A good use case rarely hands over the whole task. Decide where AI helps and where judgement and accountability stay with you.

AI could help with

Reading the ledger export and invoices, spotting anything over £250 that changed from last month, drafting the cover note

Summarise, compare, sort, extract, draft, reformat, spot patterns

Ana still has to

Check every figure against the ledger, decide what to say about disputes, approve and send

Give context, check facts, decide, approve, handle exceptions, take responsibility

07
Tutto.Step 05
05 · 10 minutes

Quick risk check

Six flags. A "yes" doesn't rule the task out. It tells you which safeguard you need.

Does the task involveAnaSafeguard
Confidential or personal informationYesBusiness plan with training off. Tenant names only where needed.
Figures, dates or citations that must be rightYesFigures come straight from the ledger. AI writes words, you check every total.
A legal, HR, safety or regulatory decisionNo
Communication with customers or the publicYesNothing goes to a landlord without Ana reading it.
A result that's hard to checkNo
An action AI must never take aloneYesAI never sends. Ana sends.
08
Tutto.Step 06
06 · 10 minutes

What would success look like?

"Save time" isn't enough. Describe a result you'd recognise and could compare.

Today1.5 days a month. Notes go out on day 4 or 5.
TargetHalf a day. Notes out by day 2.
Quality barEvery figure matches the ledger. Every change over £250 is explained in plain English.
ChecksTotals reconcile to the ledger export before anything is sent.
ValueA day a month back, and landlords stop ringing to ask what a charge was.

Tutto.Step 07
07 · 10 minutes

Your Use Case Card

Everything from the last six steps on one card. This is the task you carry through the programme.

When the month closes, I need to write each landlord's statement note using the ledger export and maintenance invoices to produce a statement pack ready for my review.
AI does

Reads the export, flags changes over £250, drafts each note

Ana does

Checks totals, edits the notes, approves and sends

First experiment

Three landlords from last month, drafted side by side with what she sent
