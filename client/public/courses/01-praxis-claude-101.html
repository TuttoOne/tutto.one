Tutto.Lesson 1 · Meet Claude
Lesson 1

Not a chatbot — a thinking partner

A chatbot answers and you're on your own. Claude brings the intelligence; you bring the context and judgement, and you work the problem together.

Same intelligence whether you reach it on web, desktop or mobile — built on three principles: helpful, harmless, honest. The apps are just front doors.

03
Tutto.Lesson 2 · Meet Claude
Lesson 2 — if you take one thing away

Set the stage · define the task · specify the rules

Talk to it like a capable colleague. A good prompt carries three things — and your first message starts a conversation, not a one-shot.

Add context by uploading files, and let memory and styles carry your role and preferences forward so you stop repeating yourself.

04
Tutto.Lesson 3 · Meet Claude
Lesson 3

When it's off, it's almost always fixable

The first draft is a starting point. Most disappointing answers trace back to one thing: context the prompt didn't carry.

This is AI Fluency in practice — the 4D framework: Delegation, Description, Discernment, Diligence. Run a quick eval (5–10 of your own examples) to learn where Claude already fits your work.

05
Tutto.Lesson 4 · Meet Claude
Lesson 4

Three modes, matched to the size of the job

The desktop app gives you one Claude in three shapes. Pick by how big and how hands-off the work is.

Cowork and Code run on Claude Code underneath — local to your machine, able to spin up subagents and sustain long tasks. Cowork stays inside the folders you share; Code works directly in your project.

06
Tutto.Lesson 5 · Organise
Lesson 5

Projects — a workspace that remembers

Stop re-uploading the same files and re-explaining the same rules. A project holds them once; every chat inside it starts already briefed.

Best for anything ongoing rather than a one-off. Name your files descriptively — Claude uses the filename to find the right one.

07
Tutto.Lesson 6 · Organise
Lesson 6

Artifacts — the output, rendered beside the chat

Not a wall of code buried in the conversation. A finished thing in its own window: rendered, editable, ready to use.

Be specific and iterate one change at a time. If Claude answers in the chat when you wanted something usable, just say "make that an artifact".

08
Tutto.Lesson 7 · Organise
Lesson 7

Skills — packaged know-how, run the same way every time

A skill is a folder of instructions and resources Claude loads when it's relevant. Two kinds, and one clean rule of thumb.

Only install custom skills from trusted sources — they can include code. The deep dive is Anthropic's dedicated "Introduction to agent skills".

09
Tutto.Lesson 8 · Expand its reach
Lesson 8

Connectors — bring your tools into the conversation

Instead of starting cold every time, Claude works with your actual data: searching, reading, and acting across the apps you already use.

Claude sees only what you can see — connecting your email doesn't expose anyone else's. Permissions are scoped and revocable; only connect tools you trust.

10
Tutto.Lesson 9 · Expand its reach
Lesson 9

Enterprise Search — ask your whole company one question

A pre-built project over your organisation's knowledge. "What happened while I was out?" answered from across every connected source.

Team and Enterprise plans only: an admin sets it up, each person authenticates their own accounts. It returns only what you already have permission to see.

11
Tutto.Lesson 10 · Expand its reach
Lesson 10

Research — from one search to a researched report

Not a single lookup. Claude works agentically: planning, running many searches that build on each other, then writing it up with citations.

Reach for Research when a thorough answer needs many sources. For a quick fact use web search; for pure reasoning use extended thinking; for internal-only questions use Enterprise Search.

Tutto.Lesson 11 · Put it to work
Lesson 11

Whatever your role, there's a starting point

The point isn't the feature list — it's the work. Here's one concrete place to begin in each function.

Pick one recurring task this week and try it. The skill sharpens with practice — you build intuition for where Claude earns its place.

Tutto.Lesson 12 · Put it to work
Lesson 12

One intelligence, where you already work

claude.ai is one front door. The same Claude shows up inside the tools you spend your day in — each shape tuned to that surface.

Where you go next depends on the work: building software (Code), team context (Slack), models (Excel), decks (PowerPoint), or the live web (Chrome).
