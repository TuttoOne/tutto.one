Tutto.Lesson 1 · Foundation
Lesson 1

Fluency is four things at once

Not just getting AI to work — getting it to work in a way you'd defend. Effective, efficient, ethical, safe, all grounded in your mission.

For a nonprofit, every efficiency gain should translate into more impact for the people you serve. Start by writing one reusable context document about your organisation — its mission, your role, your constraints — to share into future conversations.

Tutto.Lesson 2 · Foundation
Lesson 2 — if you take one thing away

Four competencies, two loops

Delegation and diligence decide whether AI belongs. Description and discernment make the collaboration actually good.

The four interlock — you rarely run one without the others. Delegation and diligence decide whether AI belongs; description and discernment make the work good. That interplay is the fluency.

Tutto.Lesson 3 · Describe & discern
Lesson 3

Research is a loop, not a single ask

A context-rich prompt gets you oriented fast. Then you verify, notice the gaps, and ask a sharper follow-up.

AI gets you oriented quickly, but you stay the decision-maker. Flag specific claims for verification, ask it to track down the original source, and check whether a "good fit" funder is actually aligned, not just topically related.

05
Tutto.Lesson 4 · Describe & discern
Lesson 4

AI drafts faster — you keep the voice

Feed it what it can't know: your track record, your partnerships, the way you actually talk. Then discern every line.

"Make this more compelling" gets you nothing. "Add our work with the Riverside shelter, in our own plain voice" gets you a draft you can stand behind. Watch for deficit framing about the people you serve.

06
Tutto.Lesson 5 · Delegate & be diligent
Lesson 5

Decide what AI never needs to see

Most analysis doesn't need names. Strip what identifies people, match the tool to the sensitivity, then share.

Safe AI use isn't avoiding AI — it's using it responsibly. Match the tool to the task: a free account for brainstorming, a protected one for anything sensitive. If something goes wrong, delete the conversation, request deletion, and follow your protocols.

07
Tutto.Lesson 6 · Delegate & be diligent
Lesson 6

Trust it where you can already check it

Before you let AI loose on new data, test it on past data where you know the answer. Match the known result, and you've earned the confidence.

AI can help even if you're not data-savvy — it writes the formulas and reshapes messy data, as long as you keep asking it to explain. You stay accountable for whether the answer makes sense and for being transparent about AI's role.

08
Tutto.Lesson 7 · Make it real
Lesson 7

"Should AI do this?" beats "Can it?"

Sort the work first. Some tasks AI can run, some it can draft for you to check, some stay human. The sort is the whole game.

Start with Problem Awareness: list what people actually ask before automating anything. Keep all three kinds of diligence — be intentional about what you automate, review outputs before they ship, and be honest about AI's role.

09
Tutto.Lesson 8 · Make it real
Lesson 8

You are the human in the loop

AI should free you for the handwritten card, not automate it away. A short policy lets the whole team work the same way.

Avoid dependency through understanding, not avoidance: if you can explain what the AI is doing, that's healthy augmentation. Decide early what happens with the time you save — so it goes back into human work, not away from it.

Tutto.Lesson 9 · Make it real
Lesson 9

Run all four Ds on one real task this week

Pick something already waiting — a grant, a donor report, data to analyse — and take it through the loop.

Step-by-step blueprints for common nonprofit tasks — grants, donor comms, data — live at claude.com/resources. AI Fluency isn't a destination; it's a practice that keeps your mission at the centre as the tools change.
