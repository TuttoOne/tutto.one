Tutto.Lesson 1 · The framework
Lesson 1

Write your business down once

Most people retype their context into every chat. Build it once into a reusable briefing sheet, and every conversation starts already informed.

AI fluency is using AI in ways that are efficient, effective, ethical and safe. The point of the efficiency is to buy back time for the human work — better service, real relationships.

03
Tutto.Lesson 2 · The framework
Lesson 2 — if you take one thing away

The 4D Framework — two loops

An outer loop frames every job: decide what to hand off, then own what comes back. An inner loop does the work between them.

Delegation — decide what AI handles and what stays with you. Description — communicate what you need. Discernment — evaluate before you act. Diligence — take responsibility for the final result.

04
Tutto.Lesson 3 · Understand the AI
Lesson 3

What it does well, where it slips

Knowing how these models are trained tells you what to trust them with — and what to keep a closer eye on.

Three things made modern models possible: the transformer architecture, vast training data, and massive compute. Training is two stages — learn patterns, then learn to follow instructions.

05
Tutto.Lesson 4 · Understand the AI
Lesson 4

Under the hood: it guesses the next word

Same trick as your phone's keyboard, scaled up. From what's been said so far, it ranks likely next words and picks one — then repeats.

The idea is a century old — Markov, 1906; powering phone keyboards by 2010; transformers in 2017 made it fluent. It is prediction, not lookup, which is exactly why it can sound certain and still be wrong.

06
Tutto.Lesson 5 · The two loops
Lesson 5 — the inner loop

Describe well, then never skip the check

The inner loop in practice: research. Good context in, then discernment on the way out — because AI accelerates research, it does not replace your expertise.

Flag specific claims for checking — regulations, pricing, deadlines — against primary sources. The gap between what AI sees and what you know is where your judgment is irreplaceable.

07
Tutto.Lesson 6 · The two loops
Lesson 6 — the outer loop

Strip it before you share it, own it after

The outer loop with your own business data: delegate deliberately, protect what's sensitive, and stand behind whatever you act on.

Build validated approaches, not blind trust — document what works so you can replicate it. And AI helps even if you're not data-savvy: formulas, plain-language read-outs of your numbers.

08
Tutto.Lesson 7 · Put it to work
Lesson 7

Ask "should AI do this?" — not "can it?"

Before you automate anything, sort the work. The question is never capability; it's appropriateness.

This is all four D's at once: delegate by sorting, describe the system precisely, discern by testing on real cases, and apply diligence before a single output reaches a customer.

09
Tutto.Lesson 8 · Put it to work
Lesson 8

Write the policy your team can stand behind

Scale your judgment by writing it down. One page, three honest sections — so everyone works from the same understanding.

Being "the human in the loop" is more than oversight — you decide what problems AI should solve. The time it saves should free you for more human work, not less.

Tutto.Lesson 9 · Put it to work
Lesson 9

Pick one real task this week

Fluency is a habit, not a certificate. Choose something already waiting for you and run the full loop on it.

Keep a running doc of the prompts and workflows that work. Blueprints live at claude.com/resources/use-cases; go deeper with Anthropic's AI Fluency: Framework & Foundations.
