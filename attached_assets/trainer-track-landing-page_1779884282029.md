# Teach Praxis. Earn from it.

**Four sessions on top of Praxis turn you from someone who can build into someone who can teach it for a living.**

You have been through Praxis. You can build working tools with Claude Code, and you have felt how fast the people around you want the same thing. This is how you turn that into an income: you become a Praxis trainer, I find and organise the clients with you, and you keep 80% of the tuition.

## Who this is for

- You have completed Praxis and you can build, comfortably, on your own.
- People keep asking you how you did it, and you have started explaining it without being asked.
- You are a freelancer, consultant, or independent who wants a real income stream from teaching, without building a business from scratch.
- You would rather plug into a method that works than invent one.

This is not for someone brand new. Praxis comes first. You cannot teach a path you have not walked.

---

## The deal, in plain terms

1. **You complete Praxis** (the eight-session programme). That is the prerequisite.
2. **You do four more sessions** with me, the trainer track, which teach you how to *teach* it, not just how to do it.
3. **You clear the readiness bar** — a single real session, or a full mock, with me watching, followed by an honest debrief.
4. **You start teaching.** I find and organise your clients, with your help. You deliver the sessions.
5. **You keep 80% of the tuition. I keep 20%**, for finding the work, the method, the materials, and the support behind you.

---

## What the four sessions cover

**1. The craft.** The thing that makes a trainer worth more than a free video: reading the person, not delivering slides. Intake, output before theory, naming the fear in the room and sitting with it, pacing, and the voice that keeps Praxis trusted instead of salesy.

**2. The syllabus.** The spine you teach from, and how to pitch each idea for the person in front of you: the difference between AI and ordinary software, skill files as a firm's memory on disk, and the handful of objections every room raises. You learn it well enough to teach it without notes.

**3. Live rehearsal.** You run the student tutor end to end as a learner, then deliver the core of it back to me cold. We find the rough edges while it is safe to find them.

**4. Going live.** The readiness bar, an honest debrief, access to the private Praxis kit you will teach from, and exactly how clients reach you and get organised.

---

## What you get

- **The whole method.** The Praxis playbook, the framings that land, the objection answers, the student tutor. You are not starting from a blank page.
- **The private kit.** Repo access to the living materials, updated as the method improves. Your copy stays current.
- **Clients, organised.** I find and arrange the work with you. The more you bring yourself, the more you earn, because you keep 80% of all of it.
- **A hub behind you.** When you hit a build or an integration you cannot handle, you bring it back. We pick up the hard work and support the engagement, so a roadblock becomes a bigger job rather than a dead end.
- **A network.** Other trainers doing the same thing, sharing what works.

## The bar, and why it is there

You will carry the Praxis name, so there is a standard to clear before you teach anyone for money. It is not a quiz. It is a demonstration: one real session, or a full mock if no client is ready yet, with me watching, then an honest conversation about what worked and what did not.

This protects you as much as the brand. A trainer who is rushed out before they are ready loses their first clients and their confidence. We would rather get you genuinely ready.

To be straight about it: this is Praxis confirming you can teach Praxis to our standard. It is not an accredited qualification from an exam board, and we do not pretend otherwise. The honesty is the point, and it is exactly why clients trust the people who carry our name.

## Common questions

**Do I have to do Praxis first?**
Yes. The trainer track builds on it. You cannot teach a path you have not walked yourself.

**How long until I am teaching?**
Four sessions and clearing the readiness bar. Most of the timing is down to how much you practise between sessions.

**Do I have to find my own clients?**
No. I find and organise the work with you. You are welcome to bring your own as well, and because you keep 80% of everything, bringing your own simply means you earn more.

**What is the 20% actually for?**
Finding and organising your lessons, the method and its ongoing updates, the private kit you teach from, and a hub that picks up the hard builds you escalate.

**What if a client needs something I cannot build?**
You bring it back to the hub. We take on the complex work and support your engagement, and you stay the relationship the client trusts.

**Is this an accredited trainer qualification?**
No. It is Praxis confirming you can teach Praxis well. We are clear about that, because overclaiming is exactly what we teach people not to do.
