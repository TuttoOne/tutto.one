# Build your own tools with Claude Code

**An eight-session programme for people who have used AI in a chat box and want to go further.**

You have typed questions into ChatGPT or Claude and got useful answers back. This is the next step: using Claude Code to build small, working tools that do your repetitive work for you, in plain English, with someone sitting beside you who has done it before.

No coding background needed. By the end you will have built something you actually use, and you will know how to keep building on your own.

## Who this is for

- You have used AI as a chat assistant and hit the ceiling of what copy-and-paste can do.
- You have a task you do every week that you suspect a machine could do for you.
- You are not a developer, and you are not trying to become one. You want to make useful things.
- You would rather be shown than left alone with a pile of conflicting videos.

It works for professionals, teams, and anyone whose week is full of work that repeats.

## Why a person, and not another video

The internet is full of free tutorials. What a video cannot do is see where you are stuck and hear what you are unsure about. That gap, between "I have heard of Claude Code" and "I am building," is the whole reason this exists.

- **Tailored to your actual work**, not a generic example you have to translate.
- **We meet you where you are.** No burying you in tools you have never heard of to look clever.
- **The fiddly parts are where a guide earns their keep.** Installing the tool, the first setup, the moment something breaks: that is exactly the part the free videos skip, and exactly where most people quietly give up.

## What you will be able to do by the end

- Build small working tools for your own work, by describing what you want in plain English.
- Capture your own rules and judgement so a tool works the way you do, every time.
- Understand what is safe, what it costs, and what it can and cannot do.
- Hand a tool to a colleague or a developer cleanly, with no black box.
- Keep building on your own after the programme ends.

---

## The eight sessions

Weekly, over roughly two months. The first six get you to a confident, independent builder. The last two take you deeper, into the kind of work that usually needs a developer.

**1. Foundations and your first build.** See it work before any theory. Understand where AI is brilliant and where it has to be exact, whether your files are safe, and what it costs. Build your first small working thing, live.

**2. The build loop, and capturing your know-how.** Describe what you want and watch Claude Code build it. Then write down the rules your work follows, in plain English, so the tool applies them every single time. Build a real tool for your own work.

**3. Your tools and surfaces.** The Claude app and the move into your own files. Connecting Claude to the apps you already use. Letting it do safe work for you, with you in control of what it is allowed to touch.

**4. Keeping versions, and safe handover.** How to keep versions of your work, hand a tool to a colleague or developer cleanly, and build so it is still changeable in a year rather than a tangle you cannot undo.

**5. How your tools talk to other software.** What you are paying for and what you are not. What an API is, in plain terms. Keeping everything in one place instead of a knot of half-connected apps. And what "training an AI" actually means, so you can stop worrying your data is being swallowed.

**6. Build something real, end to end.** A full build for your own work, mostly driven by you, with me reading the room. You finish the core programme with a tool you use and a certificate that shows what you built.

**7. Going deeper.** Fixing things when they break. Bigger, multi-part builds. And whichever surface fits your work: design, the web, your documents.

**8. A first integration, and your final build.** Connect your tool to something else you use. Understand hosting and automation at a level you can act on. Ship your final build.

## Common questions

**Do I need to know how to code?**
No. You describe what you want in plain English. Claude Code writes the code. We use the time to make sure you understand what you are building and why.

**I have only ever used AI in a chat box. Is this for me?**
Yes. That is exactly the starting point this is built for.

**Is my data safe?**
Your files stay on your own machine. You choose the folder Claude Code works in, and that folder is the boundary. It asks before going any further. For regulated or sensitive work, we map your IT and compliance questions before touching anything.

**What will it cost me to run afterwards?**
About twenty dollars a month for the subscription. The tools you build run locally and do not charge per use.

**What if I get stuck between sessions?**
You get a practice task and a clear way to get unstuck. When something breaks, the fix is usually one screenshot away, and learning that habit is part of the programme.

**One-to-one or a group?**
Both are available. Tell me which suits you and we will shape it accordingly.
